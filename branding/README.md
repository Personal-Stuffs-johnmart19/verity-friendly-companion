# Project avatar

`verity-companion-icon.png` is an AI-generated project illustration made with the
built-in imagegen tool, using the in-game Verity appearance as a visual reference.
It is not a gameplay screenshot. Original Verity remains a separate required mod.
The rejected voxel mascot draft is not included.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a square project-avatar illustration matching the in-game Verity:
> a smooth yellow sphere, two small solid-black vertical oval eyes without
> highlights, and a broad thin slightly irregular black crescent smile. Preserve
> the simple proportions, matte yellow surface and olive lower shadow. Center
> the floating ball on a muted blue-green background with a faint block-world
> silhouette. No HUD, chat, player hand, text, pedestal, speech bubble, accessories,
> particles, logos or watermark. Preserve the character rather than redesigning
> it as a cute voxel mascot. Opaque square PNG.
